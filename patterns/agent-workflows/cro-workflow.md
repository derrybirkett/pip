# CRO Workflow

**Workflow Type**: Agent Role Workflow  
**Primary User**: CRO  
**Purpose**: Guide revenue-focused decisions around pricing, packaging, funnels, and retention.

## Core Responsibilities

- Evaluate pricing and packaging changes
- Monitor funnel conversion and expansion signals
- Partner with CMO on acquisition and messaging
- Partner with CPO on monetization requirements

- [Planning Pattern](./planning-pattern.md) for pricing and launch sequencing
- [Reflection Pattern](./reflection-pattern.md) for campaign and funnel analysis

  coordination
